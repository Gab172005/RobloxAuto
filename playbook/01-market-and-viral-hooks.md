# Chapter 01 — Market Signals, Rising Genres & Viral Hooks

> Mapped to: `Lead`, `Pulse`, `Splash`

## 1. How the Roblox Discovery Algorithm Works
Roblox Home Recommendations optimize for a weighted composite of four live signals:
1. **Qualified Play-Through Rate (qPTR / CTR)**: Percentage of impressions that click and remain past initial load. Target **3.0%–5.0%+**.
2. **Playtime & Session Length**: Minutes played per visit. Target **12–20+ min** average session.
3. **Retention (D1 / D7 / D30)**: Returning users without external ads. Target **D1 >= 13%**, **D7 >= 4%**, **D30 >= 2%**.
4. **Monetization Efficiency (ARPDAU & Payer %)**: Games generating healthy Robux/user receive higher recommendation value.

**Key Insight**: A studio with zero ad budget can break out organically if its **thumbnail + title CTR** earns clicks AND its **first 120 seconds** convert clicks into 15-minute sessions.

## 2. High-ROI Genre Selection Matrix (Solo / AI-Factory Fit)
Prioritize genres with **high replayability, low custom-rig art debt, mobile-first controls, and built-in social clout**:

| Genre Archetype | Mobile Fit | Monetization Ceiling | Art Debt | Viral Clip Potential |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **RNG / Roll + Base Flex / Co-op Collector** | Excellent | Very High (Luck passes, Server Luck) | Low–Med | High (0.001% mythic roll reactions) |
| **Tycoon-Simulator Hybrid (Active + Passive)** | Excellent | Very High (Auto-Collect, 2x Cash, Rebirths) | Low–Med | Medium–High (mega base reveals) |
| **Fast Round Chaos / Survival / Brainrot Arena** | Excellent | High (Troll/Event DevProducts, Cosmetics) | Low | Very High (10s chaotic clips) |
| **Co-op Escape / Extraction / Horror-Lite** | Good | High (Revives, Loadout Passes, Server Events) | Med | Very High (jump-scare & clutch saves) |
| **Incremental Digging / Fishing / Crafting** | Excellent | Very High (Auto-fish/dig, Storage, Luck) | Low | High (giant rare catch reveals) |

**Red-Ocean Traps to Avoid**:
- Generic stair/lava obbies with zero progression or meta-loop (low D1, weak monetization).
- Massive open-world anime RPGs requiring 50+ bespoke rigged animations and combat netcode before MVP.
- Unlicensed third-party copyrighted IP (DMCA takedown wipes out all DevEx investment overnight).

## 3. Idea Scoring Rubric (0–100)
`Pulse` scores every concept across 6 weighted pillars:
- **Trend Momentum (0–20)**: Rising search keywords, growing sub-genre CCU, fresh twist on a proven loop.
- **First-2-Minute Hook (0–20)**: Zero-text clarity, instant action within 5–10s, first dopamine payout under 30s.
- **Mobile Fit (0–15)**: Smooth 2-thumb gameplay on 667x375, minimal precision aiming, low draw calls.
- **Monetization Depth (0–20)**: Clean fit for Impulse (49–199 R$), Permanent Passes (199–499 R$), and Whale/Social Sinks (Server Boosts, Gifting, Season Pass).
- **Viral Clip Potential (0–15)**: Built-in shareable moments (giant scale, huge rarity popups, funny physics fails, server-wide events).
- **Build Complexity Fit (0–10)**: Ship a playable, polished MVP within 1–2 studio cycles.

**Go/No-Go Thresholds**:
- **80–100**: Greenlight immediately into full factory build.
- **65–79**: Refine the hook or add a stronger social/monetization twist before building.
- **< 65**: Pivot or discard.

## 4. First-5-Second Thumbnail & Title CTR Rules
Players scroll the Roblox Home feed at thumb speed—thumbnails render as small as **150px wide** on phones:
- **Rule of One Subject**: One huge focal subject filling 50%+ of the frame with strong silhouette separation against a bright, clean background.
- **High-Contrast Complementary Color Pairs**: Cyan/Orange, Electric Purple/Gold, Lime/Magenta. Never muddy dark grey.
- **Emotion or Contrast Story**: Show either *extreme rarity/scale* ("TINY vs GIANT"), *active danger/chaos*, or *instant fantasy payoff*.
- **Zero Paragraph Text**: 0 to 2 giant bold words maximum (outline + drop shadow), NEVER placed in the bottom corners where Roblox overlays UI badges.
- **Always Test 3 Distinct Variants** using Roblox Thumbnail Personalization (not minor color tweaks—test 3 genuinely different compositions).

## 5. Engineering Viral TikTok / Reels / Shorts Moments Into Gameplay
Do not treat marketing as an afterthought—design 1–2 **clip-bait mechanics** straight into the core game:
- **Server-Wide Spectacle**: When someone buys a Server Boost or unlocks a 1-in-10,000 item, shift sky lighting, fire a huge beam into the sky, and show a global banner.
- **Escalating Risk/Reward**: Push-your-luck mechanics ("Keep climbing for 10x or cash out now?") naturally create 15-second suspense videos.
- **Absurd Visual Scaling**: Items, pets, or bases that physically grow massive or golden as players upgrade create instant visual hooks for short-form video.
